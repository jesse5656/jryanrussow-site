## Cross-Case Questions from Challenger

The established Challenger case raises the following questions for independent
cross-case testing:

1. Does relevant institutional knowledge commonly exist before failure without
   becoming fully available or usable at the decision point?

2. Are information distribution, severity representation, and escalation to
   authority distinct organizational failure modes?

3. Under what conditions does independent safety or assurance provide effective
   redundancy against operational-management error?

4. Does repeated successful operation in the presence of known anomaly alter
   how the anomaly is treated operationally?

5. How should institutional capacity pressure be distinguished from direct
   decision coercion?

6. Why can one institution act effectively on some hazards while failing to act
   effectively on another?

7. Are institutional possession of knowledge and decision availability of
   knowledge empirically distinct states?

8. Does Selective Conversion Failure recur in an independent case, or should
   the hypothesis be revised or rejected?

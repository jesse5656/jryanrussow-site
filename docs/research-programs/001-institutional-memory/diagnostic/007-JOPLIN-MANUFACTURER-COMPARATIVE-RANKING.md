## Ranking Method

Tier precedence controls first. Within a tier, the Version 1.1 tie-breakers are
applied in order:

1. completeness of the P-A-C-R pattern;
2. evidence quality and independence;
3. boundability;
4. urgency;
5. sponsor identifiability;
6. economic fit;
7. likely evidence access;
8. follow-on potential.

No points or hidden numerical score were used.

## 1 — Protein Solutions

**Why first:** Protein Solutions is the only Moderate candidate. The dossier
supports a current Joplin boundary, a material historical odor-related signal,
organizational awareness, company-stated mitigation activity, and later
official DNR odor concerns.

The decisive limitation remains substantial: the 2025 DNR investigation
observed no odor-rule violation, and the later concerns do not establish
verified plant-caused post-correction recurrence. Therefore this ranking does
not convert the candidate to Strong.

**Human-review questions that control the decision:**

- What specific corrective actions, engineering changes, maintenance standards,
  and effectiveness checks followed the historical odor problem?
- Do internal complaint and monitoring records show comparable recurrence after
  those actions, and how was source attribution verified?
- Can one current sponsor provide the bounded evidence/interviews and authorize
  an approximately-$10,000 pilot?

**Disposition:** first human-qualification priority; not selected and not
outreach-ready.

## 2 — National Manufacturing Group / Able Composites

**Why second:** The current Joplin plant and one discrete LOTO/energy-control
program are strongly bounded. Primary OSHA records establish specific serious
LOTO deficiencies and documented February 2025 abatement. Two 2026 OSHA
complaint inspections create a current attention signal.

The Strong/Moderate barrier is decisive: those 2026 matters remain open and do

Among the two Weak candidates, National Manufacturing Group ranks above Dyno

the open OSHA matters are violations.

**Disposition:** conditional backup for human qualification, especially if
later official evidence resolves the 2026 hazard-comparability question.

## 3 — Dyno Nobel — Carthage Plant

**Why third:** Dyno Nobel has rich public evidence, strong current-site
continuity, and a highly bounded environmental-control history. Those strengths
do not compensate for the core counterevidence.

The strongest later evidence favors successful correction and closure of the
2020 environmental matter: reported completion of remaining consent-decree
requirements, ISO 14001 audit performance, and 2025 decree termination. Later
OSHA events concern materially different occupational-safety lineages and
cannot be used to manufacture environmental recurrence.

**Disposition:** do not prioritize from the current public dossier. Reconsider
only if human qualification or new permitted evidence identifies a current
recurring problem that is actually connected to a present Carthage boundary.

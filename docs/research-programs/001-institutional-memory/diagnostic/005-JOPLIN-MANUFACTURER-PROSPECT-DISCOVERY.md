## Discovery Dispositions

### Advance to governed dossier development

#### Protein Solutions

Public evidence supports an odor-related problem lineage, organizational
awareness, stated mitigation activity, subsequent complaints or regulatory
attention, and a currently operating Joplin boundary.

The independently verified June 2022 Missouri incident involved an animal-fat
spill and does not establish comparable recurrence of the odor problem.
Comparable post-action recurrence therefore remains unresolved.

**Disposition:** Advance for a complete dossier because several P-A-C-R
elements appear supportable, while recurrence comparability requires strict
resolution.

#### National Manufacturing Group / Able Composites

A 2024 OSHA inspection of Able Composites produced serious citations. National
Manufacturing Group has later 2026 OSHA inspections associated with the same
Joplin address. Public acquisition and company material supports a continuity
inquiry between Able Composites and National Manufacturing Group.

The later inspections remain open in the reviewed evidence, and their hazard
comparability has not been established.

**Disposition:** Advance for a complete dossier focused on entity, site,
process, and event continuity. Do not treat the open inspections as recurrence
unless later official evidence establishes comparability.

#### Dyno Nobel

Public evidence supports multiple regulatory and safety events, corrective
actions or settlements, and a currently operating Carthage boundary.

The identified later events concern materially different hazards. The reviewed
evidence does not establish comparable recurrence after a documented
corrective action.

**Disposition:** Advance for a complete dossier only if the source packet can
separate the environmental and occupational-safety lineages without implying a
shared cause.

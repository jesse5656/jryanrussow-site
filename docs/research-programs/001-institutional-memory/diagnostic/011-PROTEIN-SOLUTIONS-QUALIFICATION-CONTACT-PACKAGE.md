## 6. Qualification Dimensions

Each dimension must be answered or explicitly recorded as unresolved.

Questions may be combined naturally.

### Dimension 1 — Current Problem Suitability

Is there a current or recently recurring odor-related complaint,
control-effectiveness, or complaint-response issue that leadership considers
consequential enough to examine?

### Dimension 2 — Independent Diagnostic Willingness

Would leadership consider an independent diagnostic of how information about
those concerns moves from signal through response, corrective action,
verification, and organizational learning?

### Dimension 3 — Contrary Result Acceptance

Would the inquiry remain acceptable and useful if the evidence ultimately
showed that the organization's existing controls are working effectively?

### Dimension 4 — Relevant Record Availability

Are relevant complaint, response, corrective-action, maintenance, monitoring,
and effectiveness-verification records available for a bounded evidence period?

### Dimension 5 — Evidence Shareability

Could a bounded set of those records potentially be shared with The Russow
Institute under appropriate confidentiality terms?

### Dimension 6 — Treatment of Record Gaps

If genuine historical record gaps exist, would leadership permit those gaps to
remain part of the diagnostic evidence rather than recreating records for the
engagement?

### Dimension 7 — Participant Availability

Could relevant management, environmental / EHS, maintenance, operational, and
frontline perspectives participate within approximately eight interviews?

### Dimension 8 — Sponsor Authority

Is there a sponsor with authority to:

- permit appropriate evidence access;
- permit interviews;
- receive findings that may contradict existing assumptions;
- and act on recommendations?

### Dimension 9 — Bounded Scope Fit

Can the engagement realistically remain within:

- one Joplin facility;
- one principal problem;
- approximately 75 substantive records;
- approximately 8 interviews;
- approximately 3–4 weeks;
- and approximately 70 Institute labor hours?

### Dimension 10 — Commercial Fit

If the other qualification conditions are satisfied, is approximately $10,000
a commercially realistic pilot amount?

### Dimension 11 — Excluded Professional Work

Is the organization seeking an organizational diagnostic rather than:

- legal opinion;
- engineering or regulatory certification;
- financial or accounting audit;
- licensed forensic investigation;
- or another excluded professional service?

### Dimension 12 — Non-Certification / No Predetermined Conclusion

Does leadership understand that the Institutional Memory Diagnostic:

- does not certify regulatory compliance; and
- does not begin with a predetermined conclusion?

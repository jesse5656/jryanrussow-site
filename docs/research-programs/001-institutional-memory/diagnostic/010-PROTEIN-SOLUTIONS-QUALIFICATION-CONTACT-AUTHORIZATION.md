## 3. Why Contact Is Authorized

The committed public-evidence record is sufficient to justify a limited
qualification conversation because:

- Protein Solutions is the highest-ranked candidate in the bounded prospect run;
- it is the only Moderate candidate;
- the current Joplin operating boundary is supported;
- a one-site odor-mitigation / complaint-response boundary is provisionally
  testable;
- material public problem, awareness, mitigation, and later concern signals
  exist;
- the public record cannot resolve decisive private qualification conditions.

Contact is therefore needed to resolve suitability, evidence access, personnel,
sponsor authority, willingness, scope, exclusions, and economics.

The contact is not intended to prove the prospect theory.

## 6. Qualification Questions

The contact shall resolve, or explicitly leave unresolved, the twelve
decision-blocking questions from the pre-contact brief.

### Current Problem and Suitability

1. Is there a current or recently recurring odor-related complaint,
   control-effectiveness, or complaint-response issue that leadership considers
   consequential enough to examine?
2. Would leadership consider an independent diagnostic of how information about
   those concerns moves from signal through response, corrective action,
   verification, and learning?
3. Is the proposed inquiry acceptable even if the evidence ultimately shows
   that existing controls are working effectively?

### Evidence Access

4. Are complaint, response, corrective-action, maintenance, monitoring, and
   effectiveness-verification records available for a bounded evidence period?
5. Can those records be shared with the Institute under appropriate
   confidentiality terms?
6. Are known gaps or unavailable records acceptable as part of the diagnostic
   evidence rather than being recreated for the engagement?

### Personnel and Sponsor Authority

7. Can relevant management, environmental/EHS, maintenance, operational, and
   frontline perspectives participate within an approximately eight-interview
   limit?
8. Is there a sponsor with authority to provide evidence access, permit
   interviews, receive potentially contradictory findings, and act on
   recommendations?

### Scope and Economics

9. Can the work remain limited to one site, one principal problem, approximately
   75 substantive records, approximately eight interviews, approximately
   3–4 weeks, and approximately 70 Institute labor hours?
10. Is an approximately-$10,000 pilot commercially acceptable if the
    qualification and scope conditions are satisfied?

### Exclusions

11. Is the organization seeking an organizational diagnostic rather than a
    legal opinion, engineering or regulatory certification, financial/accounting
    audit, licensed forensic investigation, or another excluded professional
    service?
12. Does leadership understand that the engagement does not certify regulatory
    compliance and does not begin with a predetermined conclusion?

Questions may be combined naturally in conversation, but each dimension must
ultimately be answered or recorded as unresolved.

## 7. Contact Conduct Boundary

The initial contact and any qualification conversation shall:

- use neutral, non-accusatory language;
- state that the purpose is pilot qualification;
- avoid claiming that odor concerns prove current control failure;
- avoid claiming regulatory noncompliance;
- avoid presenting the prospect tier as an organizational diagnosis;
- ask about evidence availability and shareability without requesting a
  substantive evidence production before qualification;
- keep the approximately-$10,000 commercial boundary explicit when appropriate;
- remain within one site and one principal problem;
- remain human-directed.

Reasonable human follow-up for routing, scheduling, or completing the bounded
qualification conversation is permitted.

Automated sequences, bulk prospecting, and generalized sales campaigns are not
authorized.

## 8. Required Post-Contact Disposition

After the bounded qualification conversation, assign one disposition using the
Pilot Authorization Package vocabulary.

### QUALIFIED

Use only if the organization confirms a consequential current bounded problem,
realistic evidence access, relevant participant availability, suitable sponsor
authority, willingness to permit contradictory findings, viable pilot
economics, and no disqualifying excluded-service request.

### QUALIFIED WITH CONDITIONS

Use if core fit is supported but manageable evidence, personnel, scope, timing,
or commercial conditions remain.

### DEFER

Use if the organization may be suitable but current timing, problem,
evidence access, sponsor availability, or economics do not support a pilot now.

### DECLINE

Use if the organization lacks a suitable bounded problem, cannot provide the
necessary evidence or personnel, requires excluded professional work, demands
validation of a predetermined conclusion, or cannot fit the pilot boundary.

None of these dispositions automatically selects or authorizes the pilot.

## Temporal Research Architecture

BP sources will be classified as:

1. **Pre-event**
2. **Immediate / early investigation**
3. **Final investigation**
4. **Independent post-accident review**
5. **Longitudinal corrective / enforcement history**

This separation is mandatory.

Later evidence must not silently overwrite what was actually known before the
event.

## Establishment Standard

A Challenger finding may advance only when all of the following are true:

1. the proposition is supported by the structured primary-source evidence;
2. material qualifying evidence is preserved;
3. the wording does not exceed the source record;
4. counterfactual certainty is excluded;
5. technical uncertainty is preserved where relevant;
6. the finding is sufficiently distinct from the other findings;
7. the finding is bounded to the supported organizational domain;
8. the finding stands independently of Selective Conversion Failure;
9. the finding does not require an Institutional Memory framework proposition;
10. evidence-ID traceability is intact.

# Establishment Review

## CF-01 — Relevant Technical Knowledge Existed Before 51-L

**Classification**

Direct historical finding

**Review**

The record directly establishes substantial pre-51-L technical knowledge
concerning the field joint and O-rings while preserving uncertainty concerning
universal individual awareness and exact failure prediction.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CF-02 — Higher-Level Decision Channels Did Not Receive a Complete Representation of Existing Knowledge

**Classification**

Organizational-process finding

**Review**

The revised proposition is supported by direct evidence of nonreceipt,
nonforwarding, incomplete representation, and differing awareness among
important decision channels.

It no longer makes the universal claim that all higher-level officials lacked
the information.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CF-03 — O-Ring Risk Was Represented Inconsistently

**Classification**

Organizational-process finding

**Review**

The revised finding is directly supported by inconsistencies in criticality,
risk representation, anomaly treatment, management reporting, launch
constraints, and readiness information.

The wording concerns representation rather than intent.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CF-04 — Escalation Did Not Reliably Place Material O-Ring Risk Information Before Higher-Level Authorities

**Classification**

Organizational-process finding

**Review**

The revised wording removes the absolute phrase "complete risk picture" and
limits the proposition to material concerns, anomaly history, and
launch-constraint information actually supported by the evidence.

The finding does not claim that better escalation necessarily would have
prevented Challenger.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CF-05 — Safety Architecture Did Not Provide Effective Redundancy for the O-Ring Risk

**Classification**

Organizational-process finding

**Review**

Chapter VII directly establishes failures in independence, staffing,
expertise, reporting, trend analysis, and participation relevant to the O-ring
problem.

Chapter IX counterevidence prevents generalization to every NASA safety domain.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CF-06 — Repeated Success Coexisted With Increasing Acceptance of O-Ring Anomalies

**Classification**

Direct historical finding

**Review**

The historical pattern is directly supported by the Commission record and does
not depend on the term "normalization of deviance."

Technical uncertainty and legitimate engineering rationale remain explicit
qualifiers.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CF-07 — Schedule and Resource Pressure Formed a Constrained Operating Environment

**Classification**

Contextual finding

**Review**

The revised wording states only effects directly documented by the Commission:
compressed training, extreme workload, limited resource margin, displacement
of long-term work, spare-parts shortages, and reduced time for anomaly review.

It does not claim direct schedule causation of the January 27–28 launch
decision.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH AS CONTEXT**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CF-08 — NASA Recognized and Acted on Hazards in Other Shuttle Safety Domains

**Classification**

Comparative / counterevidence finding

**Review**

The revised wording is behavioral rather than theoretical.

It is retained to prevent an unsupported conclusion that NASA was universally
incapable of recognizing hazards or taking corrective action.

Chapter IX remains explicitly outside the physical causal chain of 51-L.

**Establishment recommendation**

**ESTABLISH AS COUNTEREVIDENCE**

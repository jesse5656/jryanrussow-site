# Atomic Observations

## BP-E-001 — The incident occurred during startup of the ISOM raffinate splitter

**Observation**

On March 23, 2005, the ISOM raffinate splitter tower was overfilled during
startup. Pressure relief devices opened and discharged flammable liquid into a
blowdown system that vented to atmosphere. The resulting flammable vapor cloud
ignited, causing the explosion and fire.

**Source**

- BP-SRC-002
- PDF pages 17, 21–22
- Sections 1.1 and 1.3

**Evidence type**

Technical condition / incident reconstruction

**Temporal position**

Event

## BP-E-002 — The blowdown system was an old atmospheric-discharge design

**Observation**

The CSB describes the ISOM blowdown drum and stack as an antiquated design
installed in the 1950s and not connected to a flare system. During the incident,
flammable liquid discharged through this system and formed the vapor cloud.

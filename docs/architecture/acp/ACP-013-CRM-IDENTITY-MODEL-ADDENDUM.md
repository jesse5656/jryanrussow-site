## Approved target identities and lifecycle ownership

1. **Lead:** Add the smallest Midwest24-owned `M24Lead` (`leadId`, immutable, opaque, generated by Enterprise). It is one distinct intake/prospect *event*, not a Party identity. A person/customer may have many Leads; a Lead may initially lack a matched Party. `M24Lead.currentStatusId` is a projection of an append-only `M24LeadStatusEvent` (`leadStatusEventId`, `leadId`, from/to status, controlled reason, event time, initiating human, effective executor, source/correlation and decision evidence). Govern status and reason semantics by Capability 001; do not invent a second taxonomy. An owned effective-dated `M24LeadPartyLink` (`leadId`, `partyId`, relationship role, `fromDate`, `thruDate`) relates zero or more contacts/customers without duplicating their identities. Role, merge, correction and unlink events remain auditable. Preserve original source payload reference/hash and intake time, never secrets or an unbounded payload as the Lead key.
2. **Property:** Use native `Facility.facilityId` with `FacilityType=M24_PROPERTY`. The separate Property decision governs effective-dated `FacilityParty` owner/occupant/contact roles, `FacilityContactMech`/`PostalAddress` service-address history, native `WorkEffort.facilityId` on the one-property A16 Job and Work Orders, and owned `M24FacilityStatus` status history. `Facility.ownerPartyId` is not ownership history. Preserve append-only attributable correction evidence for address, relationship and identity changes; do not merge by address alone. Customer-to-Property access must be verified through time-valid, explicitly scoped native Party/Facility relationships, not inferred from a shared address or Lead. No multi-property Job semantics are approved by this addendum.
3. **Opportunity:** Use native `SalesOpportunity.salesOpportunityId` as the authoritative immutable key, with native `SalesOpportunityHistory` for stage/field transitions, `SalesOpportunityRole` for *current* Party roles and `SalesOpportunityWorkEffort` for downstream Job links. Because `SalesOpportunityRole` has no dates, an owned effective-dated `M24OpportunityPartyLink` is required for historical/customer-contact roles; current native roles are a validated projection. The Property decision's owned `M24SalesOpportunityFacility(salesOpportunityId, facilityId, associationTypeId, fromDate, thruDate)` supplies the direct Property relation and history. One Party or Property may relate to many Opportunities; an Opportunity may cover multiple Parties/Properties only with explicit scoped links. Stage values remain governed separately; do not infer a production sales lifecycle from OFBiz defaults.

`M24LeadConversion(leadId, conversionEventId)` records executed-contract reference, signature/validation evidence, actor/time, related Opportunity and optional resulting Job; it may be written only when BR-038 is met. A Lead may have zero or one current valid conversion outcome, with immutable correction/reversal events rather than reuse or deletion. Opportunity creation, Party role assignment or a qualified status alone never convert it. Until a record-family cutover is separately approved, EspoCRM remains the operational status/record authority; Enterprise synthetic statuses and links are candidate representations only.

## Source identity, authorization and document boundary

Extend the proposed `M24CrmRecordLink` contract to key by `(sourceSystemId, sourceInstanceId, sourceRecordTypeId, sourceRecordId)`; the existing three-part key lacks the source-instance boundary. Include an exactly one-of target reference for each link type (`leadId`, `partyId`, `facilityId`, `salesOpportunityId`, or `workEffortId`), recorded `linkStateId`, immutable source payload hash, provenance time and attributable correction events. A foreign ID is provenance/idempotency only; never the Enterprise primary key. Same exact key and payload must replay the same target without duplicate effect; same key with conflicting content fails for review. Different source IDs cannot be silently merged by address, name, phone or human Job number; a reviewed identity link may refer multiple external keys to one native target with history. Exact-match resolution verifies the source instance, recorded state, target existence/type, current object scope and actor. A merely plausible match stays REVIEW_REQUIRED; an invalid target stays UNMATCHED.

Object-level authorization must cover the Lead, involved Parties, every Facility, Opportunity, Job/Work Order and action; enforce tenant/source-instance scope, time-valid role and relationship membership, and least-privilege actor checks on creation, read, update, replay and correction. A source-system credential or successful authentication alone cannot grant general access. A stale/ambiguous relation cannot authorize filing or cross-job access. Retain initiating human and effective execution principal in append-only business audit. A16 must prove positive and negative scoped paths, transaction rollback and idempotent replay in an isolated synthetic copy.

Preserve ACP-013's document split: Midwest24 Document Services owns canonical document/version IDs and bytes; Enterprise owns filing state, business relationships, authorization decisions and business audit, retaining exact document/version IDs and SHA-256 hashes. A staged intent is not a filed relationship. Filenames, filesystem paths, OCR and human-readable Job numbers are hints/business keys, never canonical identity. A native `FacilityContent` or `WorkEffortContent` must not become a second canonical binary or bypass the approved filing/byte-access checks.

## Alternatives, trade-offs and migration

| Option | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Native Party/Person with a LEAD role | Rejected: one Party may have many distinct Lead events, statuses and conversions; a role collapses those events into identity. |
| Native `CustRequest` with a new Lead type | Rejected as the authoritative Lead: it has an immutable ID and status history, but its customer-service/request and fulfillment semantics do not cleanly represent self-generated/outbound prospects, the contractual conversion event or direct Opportunity relation. Reusing it would require misleading request semantics and additional owned conversion/link history anyway. It may remain a separately linked request. |
| Upstream `sourceLeadId` only | Rejected: no Enterprise event identity, lifecycle, conversion or audit. |
| PartyGroup or PostalAddress as Property | Rejected: PartyGroup represents a legal/social Party; PostalAddress is a contact mechanism. The old one-Job fixture does not establish stable physical-property identity. |
| Native `Facility` as Property | Approved in the separate Property decision: stable ID, native temporal Party and address relations, `M24_PROPERTY` type, `M24FacilityStatus`, native Job/Work Order `facilityId`, and inventory separation. Validate the separation in A16. |
| Midwest24-owned Property entity | Deferred: it could enforce service-only semantics but duplicates native physical-place identity, address and temporal Party links. No material Facility deficiency has been demonstrated; reconsider if A16 shows unavoidable stock-facility behavior or inability to preserve property/authorization history. |
| External `sourceOpportunityId` or owned duplicate Opportunity | Rejected: native `SalesOpportunity` has durable identity, role, history and WorkEffort links; retain external ID solely as provenance. |

Historical JobNimbus reconstruction supports migration *test cases*, not a literal schema: multiple events and opportunities for one person, multiple properties per customer, address changes, repeat Jobs at a property and document/job-number associations. Before a production migration plan can assert semantic fidelity, obtain a representative, de-identified source export with stable contact/lead/job IDs, address revisions, role changes, opportunity/conversion events, repeated jobs and document references; reconcile cardinalities and missing/contradictory IDs. Do not infer these facts from board labels or synthetic fixtures. Maintain a reviewed old-property-Party → Facility crosswalk; never reuse a Party ID as a Facility ID or overwrite production history.

## Purpose

Record the governing human decision to authorize bounded organization-level
qualification contact with Protein Solutions, preserve the limits established
by the pre-contact qualification brief, and update the Operating Plan so the
repository reflects the actual current commercial gate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Approved Decision

The governing human reviewer approved the following decision on 2026-09-06:

**Protein Solutions — AUTHORIZE — BOUNDED QUALIFICATION CONTACT**

This approval authorizes only a qualification contact intended to resolve the
decision-blocking questions defined in
`009-PROTEIN-SOLUTIONS-PRE-CONTACT-QUALIFICATION-BRIEF.md`.

It does not:

- select Protein Solutions as the pilot client;
- authorize the Institutional Memory Diagnostic;
- authorize pilot kickoff;
- promote Protein Solutions above the governed Moderate analytical tier;
- establish that Protein Solutions has an Institutional Memory failure;
- establish that current odor controls are ineffective;
- authorize named-person enrichment;
- authorize bulk, sequenced, or automated outreach;
- authorize substantive evidence collection or diagnostic analysis before
  qualification;
- authorize legal, engineering, regulatory, financial, forensic, or other
  excluded professional work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Authorized Contact Boundary

Qualification contact may use current public organization-level channels such
as:

- the organization's general public contact channel;
- a published general facility or business email address;
- a published main business telephone number;
- organization-provided routing to an appropriate role.

The Institute may ask the organization to route the inquiry to an appropriate
operational or executive sponsor by role.

No proactive named-person enrichment is authorized.

If an organization representative identifies themself in the ordinary course of
the contact, that identity may be recorded only as normal engagement provenance.
No external enrichment of that person is authorized by this decision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Authorized Qualification Purpose

The contact is limited to determining whether the organization can satisfy the
Pilot Authorization Rule.

The qualification conversation shall resolve, or explicitly leave unresolved,
the decision dimensions already defined in the pre-contact brief:

1. current problem suitability;
2. willingness to consider an independent diagnostic;
3. willingness to accept a result showing current controls are effective;
4. relevant record availability;
5. ability to share relevant records;
6. treatment of known record gaps;
7. relevant participant availability;
8. sponsor authority;
9. bounded scope fit;
10. approximately-$10,000 commercial fit;
11. excluded-professional-work boundary;
12. understanding that the Diagnostic is not certification and does not begin
    with a predetermined conclusion.

Questions may be combined conversationally, but none of the decision dimensions
may be silently assumed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Contact Conduct Rules

The contact shall:

- use neutral qualification language;
- describe public evidence as the reason for considering fit, not as proof of
  current failure;
- preserve the 2025 Missouri DNR no-violation counterevidence;
- avoid accusations, regulatory conclusions, or claims of verified recurrence;
- avoid representing the Moderate prospect tier as a diagnosis;
- ask whether evidence exists and can be shared rather than requesting a
  substantive evidence production before qualification;
- preserve the approximately-$10,000 / 75-record / 8-interview / 3–4-week /
  70-hour pilot boundaries;
- remain human-directed rather than automated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Required Post-Contact Record

After a qualification contact, the repository record shall capture:

- date and contact channel;
- organization role or routing point used;
- answers or unresolved status for the qualification dimensions;
- any material contradiction or changed fact;
- whether the proposed boundary remains viable;
- whether the approximate commercial boundary remains viable;
- one qualification disposition using the Pilot Authorization Package
  vocabulary:
  - QUALIFIED;
  - QUALIFIED WITH CONDITIONS;
  - DEFER;
  - DECLINE.

A qualification disposition does not itself authorize the pilot.

A separate human decision remains required before Protein Solutions is selected
as the pilot prospect or the Pilot Authorization Rule is marked satisfied.

# IMD Prospect Intelligence V1.1 — Operator Method Addendum

**Version:** 1.1  
**Purpose:** Apply the approved IMD prospect-intelligence method to the supplied cases. This addendum does not diagnose an organization and does not authorize methodology changes.

## Current Meaningful Operating Boundary Gate

Strong Candidate requires a separate finding that a plausible, currently operating organizational system, site, business unit, process, or equivalent boundary exists and could presently support one bounded IMD engagement.

This gate is noncompensatory. Historical P-A-C-R completeness, event severity, evidence richness, publicity, organizational complexity, economic significance, or follow-on potential cannot compensate for a FAIL or UNKNOWN result.

The gate concerns plausible present commercial diagnosability. It does not require public proof that the organization will participate, provide records, authorize interviews, accept the proposed scope, or purchase an engagement.

### PASS

Public evidence supports, directly or through a bounded reasonable inference, that:

1. the relevant organization or successor currently operates;
2. a materially relevant system, site, business unit, process, or equivalent boundary currently exists;
3. the candidate problem remains meaningfully connected to that current boundary; and
4. the boundary is sufficiently identifiable to formulate one plausible present IMD pilot.

A PASS may rely on recent official or company-controlled evidence, current licenses or operational filings tied to the relevant activity, current facility or service descriptions, current regulatory inspections, current operating announcements, or multiple mutually consistent sources. Legal-entity status alone is insufficient.

### FAIL

Public evidence establishes that the relevant boundary has been closed, sold without demonstrated organizational continuity, permanently discontinued, dissolved, abandoned, or changed so materially that the historical candidate problem no longer identifies a plausible present IMD boundary.

A FAIL also applies when the only identifiable current operation is materially different from the historical system and no supported connection to the candidate problem remains.

### UNKNOWN

Available evidence neither establishes a plausible current boundary nor establishes discontinuation. UNKNOWN includes unresolved ambiguity about whether the relevant activity, site, process, crop, product line, business unit, or operating system continues. Absence of public evidence is UNKNOWN, not FAIL.

### Tier effect

- PASS permits, but does not establish, Strong; every other Strong gate must also pass.
- UNKNOWN prohibits Strong and ordinarily caps the candidate at Moderate when historical fit remains material and the missing continuity fact appears realistically qualifiable.
- UNKNOWN may support Weak or Insufficient Evidence when no useful present boundary can responsibly be formulated or the continuity gap is unlikely to be resolved economically.
- FAIL prohibits Strong and ordinarily results in Weak or Insufficient Evidence for a present engagement. Historical relevance may be preserved but cannot restore current prospect eligibility.

### Changed operations

- **Discontinued or closed:** FAIL when permanently ended; UNKNOWN when discontinuation is reported, planned, temporary, or unresolved.
- **Sold:** The former organization’s boundary fails unless a present boundary remains with it. Do not automatically treat a buyer or successor as the same candidate.
- **Reorganized:** Trace the relevant process, control responsibility, site, records, and operating ownership. Do not assume continuity or discontinuity.
- **Seasonal:** A normal off-season does not fail the gate. PASS requires evidence that the operation remains a recurring/current business activity.
- **Relocated:** A new location may pass when the relevant organizational system or process plausibly continues and the historical problem remains meaningfully connected.
- **Materially changed:** PASS only when enough of the relevant system, responsibility, process, or records continuum remains to define a present diagnostic; otherwise use FAIL or UNKNOWN according to the evidence.

Record the gate status, as-of date, rationale, supporting and contradictory claim IDs, and tier effect.

## Independent contradiction check

Reviewing supplied packet searches does not satisfy this obligation. Unless a documented NOT APPLICABLE exception applies, execute at least two independent disconfirmation queries per candidate within the existing 20-minute supplemental-research allowance:

1. One status/continuity query testing closure, sale, discontinuation, relocation, reorganization, correction, or current operation of the relevant boundary.
2. One query testing the weakest or most tier-determinative P-A-C-R claim, including closure, later compliance, non-comparability, ownership/control change, different pathway, or action/recurrence chronology.

A qualifying query must be capable of defeating or narrowing a material claim, changing its classification, breaking continuity, establishing correction/closure, or changing the tier. Reworded discovery searches and confirmation-only searches do not qualify.

Log every query using the supplied template. Stop when both query categories are complete, the strongest counterevidence is resolved or marked unresolved, and further searching is unlikely to change a material claim, boundary status, or tier within the timebox.

NOT APPLICABLE is allowed only when independent searching is technically or legally unavailable across all relevant permitted sources. Packet completeness, apparent tier stability, or an expectation of no counterevidence is not an exception.

## Material limitations

Record a limitation when it could reasonably change a material claim, P-A-C-R reconstruction, continuity determination, source-coverage interpretation, tier, pilot boundary, or transfer of evidence across jurisdictions, regulatory programs, time periods, or operating contexts.

Identify the affected claim IDs or decisions, materiality, and qualification or mitigation. Do not add irrelevant boilerplate. A cross-jurisdiction or state-implementation limitation is mandatory only when materially applicable.

## Completion gate

Do not assign or publish a final tier until every mandatory dossier section is complete and all claim/source references resolve.

If a mandatory field is absent, blank without an authorized reason, internally inconsistent, or references a nonexistent claim/source ID:

- set `completion_status: incomplete`;
- set `tier: null`;
- report `INCOMPLETE — NO FINAL TIER`;
- list every failed completion rule; and
- preserve the incomplete work for review.

Human review cannot waive missing provenance, claim lineage, P-A-C-R statuses, current-boundary status, or contradiction-search records. It may resolve substantive judgment but may not manufacture absent evidence.

Before finalization verify:

1. Every material assertion has a unique claim ID and permitted classification.
2. Every PUBLIC FACT and REASONABLE INFERENCE resolves to sources or predicate claims.
3. Every referenced source exists and contains the required provenance fields; unavailable fields are explicitly explained.
4. Claim-to-source and source-to-claim mappings are reciprocal.
5. Every source records lineage, locator, entity/site/unit association, and procedural status.
6. All P-A-C-R elements have explicit statuses, statements, and claim IDs.
7. Current-boundary status and tier effect are complete.
8. Independent contradiction research is complete or a valid exception is documented.
9. Material limitations and unknowns are recorded.
10. Human review is required for Strong, with triggers and decision status.
11. Tier rationale resolves to claim IDs and Strong-gate determinations.
